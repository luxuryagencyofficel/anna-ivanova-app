Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Anna Ivanova ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and handles information in connection with the use of our personal content management application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

The App is a personal tool used exclusively by its owner to manage and publish content on TikTok. The App accesses the following information through TikTok's API:

  • Basic account information (username, display name, profile picture)
  • Account statistics (follower count, following count, video count)
  • Video list and publishing status

2. How We Use Information

All information accessed through TikTok's API is used solely to:

  • Authenticate and verify the creator's personal TikTok account
  • Upload and publish travel and lifestyle video content to the creator's own TikTok profile
  • Monitor basic account performance metrics

We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data from third-party users. The App operates exclusively on the creator's own account.

3. Data Storage

No personal data is stored by this application beyond the OAuth access tokens required to authenticate with TikTok. These tokens are stored securely and are used solely to perform the above-described actions.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses TikTok's official Content Posting API and Login Kit. Data accessed through these services is governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy, available at tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy.

5. Data Retention

Authentication tokens are retained only as long as necessary to provide the publishing functionality. You may revoke access at any time through your TikTok account settings.

6. Contact

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at the address below.
